Automation is good at producing options and poor at owning outcomes. Someone has to own what goes out under the masthead.

That someone does not need to be an artist. They need to be willing to say no, to say why, and to be asked about it later.

## A small test

Before publishing, finish this sentence aloud: "We chose this one because". If the sentence ends with "it looked good", keep looking.

<Note mode="counterpoint" label="Counterpoint">
Speed matters too. A gate that takes three days will be bypassed. The approval step should be quick and impossible to skip.
</Note>

The goal is not to slow the machine. The goal is to keep a person in the loop at the one point where judgment cannot be automated.
