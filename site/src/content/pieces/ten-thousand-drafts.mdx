A team that pays per image learns quickly. The first week it generates freely. By the second week someone asks what the last forty images bought.

A hard cap turns that question into a habit. Each run shows its estimated cost first, then waits for a named person to approve it. Nothing is spent until the number has been seen.

<Note mode="note" label="Practice">
Estimate, cap, approve, record. In that order, every time.
</Note>

The surprising result is that quality goes up as volume goes down. With fewer drafts, each prompt gets read twice and each candidate gets a real look.

Caps also protect the record. When every paid run carries an approval reference, the question of who allowed what has a short answer.
