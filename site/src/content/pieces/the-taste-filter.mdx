People describe taste as a gift, which makes it sound like something that cannot be taught. In practice it behaves like a filter with a memory. It rejects most inputs and it remembers why.

## Three habits

First, reject out loud. A rejected candidate with a one-line reason teaches more than an approved one with none.

Second, compare in sets. A single image always looks fine. Four images side by side show which one has a point of view.

Third, keep the record. When a series drifts, the log of past decisions explains the drift and shows where to correct it.
