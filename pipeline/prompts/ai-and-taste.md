# Sample prompt pack: AI-generated content and editorial taste

Original sample material for the Slop Editorial demo. Every prompt describes abstract, non-figurative
illustration. Nothing here depicts a real person, brand or existing artwork.

## Shared blocks

Restraint block, appended to every prompt that lists it:

```text
Restraint: flat shapes, limited palette of deep blue, warm paper white and one warm accent. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no faces. Calm composition with generous empty space.
```

## Prompts

### Prompt 1 · Cover: The Endless Feed

```text
An abstract editorial cover illustration of an endless scroll: hundreds of near-identical small rectangles stacked in rows that fade into the distance, with a single rectangle near the center slightly larger and warmly lit.
[RESTRAINT]
```

### Prompt 2 · Spread: The Taste Filter

```text
An abstract illustration of a fine sieve or comb catching a few distinct shapes while a cloud of identical grey shapes passes through. Keep the visual language of the first image.
[RESTRAINT]
```

### Prompt 3 · Spread: Ten Thousand Drafts

```text
An abstract illustration of a tall stack of translucent sheets, almost all of them empty, with one sheet pulled out and held slightly apart from the stack.
[RESTRAINT]
```

### Prompt 4 · Closing: The Human Pick

```text
An abstract illustration of a simple hand-drawn circle, a single imperfect ring, placed around one shape among many tidy machine-made shapes.
[RESTRAINT]
```

### Prompt 5 · Alternate Cover: Quiet Page

```text
An abstract editorial cover of a nearly blank page with one small warm shape in the lower corner and a thin blue rule across the top.
[RESTRAINT]
```
